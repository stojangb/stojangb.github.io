Binbunlock Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026.

1. Controller

  • Name: Singame.
  • Privacy contact: singameconsulting@gmail.com.
  • Game: Binbunlock (Android).

2. Data the game collects

On your device (never leaves it): level progress, lives, per-stage times and music/sound/consent settings. Stored locally and deleted by uninstalling the game.

If you accept the consent prompt, the following is collected and shared:

  • Ads (Google AdMob): device advertising identifiers, model, OS, language, approximate country and ad interactions, to show ads (personalized if you accept, non-personalized if you reject). Without your decision, the ads SDK never starts.
  • Anonymous analytics (Google Firebase Analytics): session opens, started/completed/failed levels and bomb/fire usage, with no name or contact. Without your acceptance, no event is sent.
  • Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics): technical error data to fix it.
  • Account and cloud save (Google Play Games, if you sign in): player identifier and a copy of your progress (levels, lives, times) to keep playing on another device. Without sign-in, everything stays on your phone.
  • App integrity (Google Play Integrity, always on): technical check that the app is genuine, installed from Google Play and running on a real device. It only observes, never blocks the game, and collects no name, contact or advertising identifiers.
  • Web version (Google Analytics 4, browser only): if you accept the website's cookie notice, analytics cookies store page views, approximate country, browser, visit length and clicks on the play and become-a-tester buttons. If you reject, no analytics cookie is stored (consent mode).

If you reject, the game works the same: you will see non-personalized ads and no analytics will be sent.

3. Legal basis (EU/EEA and UK users)

The basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given or denied in the initial prompt, which you can change or withdraw at any time from Settings > Privacy inside the game. Withdrawing it does not affect the game.

4. Retention

Local data lives on your device until you uninstall. Analytics and crash data is kept for Google Firebase's standard periods. The Play Games cloud copy lives in your Google account until you delete it or sign out. The integrity check retains no personal data.

5. Third parties

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics): firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Analytics 4 (web): policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/cookies
  • Google AdMob (anuncios/ads): support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543 y/and policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Games (guardado en nube/cloud save, solo con sesión/with sign-in only): playgames.dev/about y/and policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Integrity (anti-fraude/anti-fraud, sin datos personales/no personal data): developer.android.com/google/play/integrity
  • Google Play (distribution): its own policy when downloading the game.

We do not sell your data.

6. Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability, and withdrawing consent, by writing to singameconsulting@gmail.com. You can also complain to your supervisory authority (in Spain, the AEPD: aepd.es).

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 without adult supervision. If you are a parent/guardian and believe your child gave us data, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Changes

We will publish any change here with its date. Continued use after a change implies acceptance; if you disagree, uninstall the game.
