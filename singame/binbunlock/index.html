🔥 The game

All aboard Binbunlock, the little train that could! Place bombs, shoot fire and glide on steam to beat 12 hand-made physics levels and reach each stage key. Every bomb you pick up is your checkpoint… and every blast is a boost to fly further.

💣

Bombs that save and boost

Each bomb marks your respawn point. Detonate it close by and the blast multiplies your jump.

🔥

Fire that solves puzzles

Burn obstacles, trigger mechanisms and chain streaks to recover flight mid-stage.

💨

Steam to glide

One flight per stage: spend it at the right moment to cross spikes, ice and timers.

🛤️ The journey, stage by stage

  1. 1–4 · No timer: learn to jump, bomb and glide.
  2. 5–8 · The timer kicks in (60″→30″), plus ice and spikes.
  3. 9–11 · Weird gravity, tight timers and zones that demand aim.
  4. 10 · 1-2-3 cone puzzle that only opens in order.
  5. 12 · Grand finale: spinning half-moons and a rollercoaster to the goal.

Stuck? Breathe, aim the bomb well and try again, engineer.
