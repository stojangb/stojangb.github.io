🔥 The game

All aboard Binbunlock, the little train that could! Clear 12 hand-made physics levels: place bombs, shoot fire, glide on steam and solve puzzles to reach each stage key. Out of lives? Earn more by watching a video.

  • 12 hand-made levels, from easy-peasy to infernal
  • Real physics: gravity, ice, spikes, timers and weird zones
  • Bombs double as checkpoints, and blasts boost your jumps
  • Rollercoaster, spinning half-moons and a cone puzzle in later stages
  • Free, no in-app purchases, rewarded ads only

Stuck? Breathe, aim the bomb well and try again, engineer.
